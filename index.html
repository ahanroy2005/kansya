The Alchemy of Dhokra

The lost-wax or lost-wax technique is an intricate balance of natural elements. Starting with a clay core, the artisans meticulously wrap thin strings of beeswax to form the design. This is then enveloped in layers of clay to create a mold, baked in an open fire, and cast with molten bell metal.

Every piece is singular; the mold must be broken to reveal the artifact within, meaning no two pieces are ever identical.
