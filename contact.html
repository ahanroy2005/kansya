Frequently Asked Questions

How long is the casting cycle?

The entire lost-wax process for a single piece can take anywhere from 15 to 45 days, depending on the complexity and size. Large totemic pieces or elaborate ritual vessels require significant drying times for the clay molds before firing.

Can I visit the foundry?

Yes, we arrange guided visits to our partnered foundries in Dhenkanal, Odisha during the winter months (November to February). Please reach out via the transmission form to inquire about availability.

Maintenance of Dhokra items?

Bell metal naturally oxidizes over time, developing a rich, dark patina that many collectors desire. If you prefer to maintain the golden hue, gently polish the piece with a soft cloth and a traditional mixture of tamarind and salt, or a specialized brass polish. Avoid harsh chemicals.
